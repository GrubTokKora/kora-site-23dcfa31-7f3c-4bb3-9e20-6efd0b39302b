Understanding Anxiety in Adults

Anxiety is more than feeling stressed or nervous. It can take over your thoughts, your body, and your ability to function. Many adults describe anxiety as constantly waiting for something to go wrong, even when everything seems fine.

Common signs of anxiety include:

  • Constant worry or racing thoughts
  • Difficulty sleeping or waking up unrested
  • Fatigue or low energy
  • Muscle tension, headaches, stomach discomfort
  • Feeling on edge, irritable, or overwhelmed
  • Trouble concentrating or making decisions
  • Panic attacks or sudden waves of fear
  • Avoiding social situations or responsibilities
  • Feeling like you’re “never doing enough”

Anxiety can be triggered by work stress, parenting pressure, relationship issues, health concerns, or unresolved emotional patterns from childhood. No matter the cause, you don’t have to face it alone.

Recognizing Signs of Depression in Adults

Depression often shows up quietly at first—low motivation, exhaustion, or feeling “off.” Over time, these feelings can grow heavier, making everyday life feel harder. Common signs include persistent sadness or emptiness; loss of interest in hobbies, relationships, or daily activities; changes in sleep or appetite; difficulty focusing; hopelessness, guilt, or low self-esteem; feeling disconnected, numb, or withdrawn; irritability; and physical symptoms like pain, tension, or fatigue.

Depression can make you feel isolated, but support at RZ Therapy Solutions is available. With the right therapy, healing is absolutely possible.
