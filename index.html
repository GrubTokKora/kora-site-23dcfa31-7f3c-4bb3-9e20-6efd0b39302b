RZ Therapy Solutions PLLC is a private counseling practice centrally located in Colchester, CT. We are dedicated to providing high-quality, developmentally appropriate mental health care to children, adolescents, adults, and their families.

Our practice is known for its comprehensive approach and strong clinical integrity. We offer a safe, supportive, and compassionate space where you can feel understood, genuinely cared for, and empowered to heal, grow, and live authentically.

Starting Your
Journey With Us

We know that reaching out for support takes courage. Our intake process is designed to be simple, warm, and low-pressure. We'll work with you to find the right therapist and the right fit for your needs.

📋 Step 1: Request
Fill out our brief online intake form. Tell us a little about yourself and what you're looking for — there's no pressure and no commitment required.
📞 Step 2: Connect
A member of our team will reach out to answer your questions, discuss availability, and match you with the right therapist for your situation.
🌱 Step 3: Begin
Schedule your first session — in our Colchester office or via secure telehealth. Your path toward healing starts here, at your own pace.
