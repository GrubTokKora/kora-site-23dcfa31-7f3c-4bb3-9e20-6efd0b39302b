Our mission

Specialized therapy for children, teens, and their families

We're so glad you found us. At RZ Therapy Solutions, we believe every child, teen, and family deserves care that truly sees them—trauma-informed, neurodiversity-affirming, and always child-centered. We're here for children, adolescents, and their families, and we also walk alongside adults through the deeply personal experiences of pregnancy, postpartum, and parenting. Our hope is that every family who comes through our door leaves feeling more empowered, more connected, and more ready to grow.

We meet you exactly where you are. Our space is warm, playful, and genuinely welcoming—because we want kids to feel safe enough to be themselves, and we want families to feel truly supported, not just seen.

We're here to walk with you every step of the way. — Rebecca Ziskind, Owner, RZ Therapy Solutions

How we care

How we support children and families

Evidence-based, child-centered care shaped around connection, safety, and the whole family—not a one-size-fits-all model.

Play-based & Child-centered

Using Child Centered Play Therapy (CCPT), we meet children in their natural language—play—creating a safe space for expression, healing, and growth.

Neurodiversity-affirming

We honor different ways of thinking, feeling, and being—helping children and teens understand their brains rather than trying to "fix" them.

Family & Attachment-focused

Through PCIT, CPP, and the ARC model, we strengthen the parent-child relationship and build the secure attachment that children need to flourish.

Evidence-based & Developmentally Adaptive

We draw on CBT and other proven approaches, always tailoring our methods to each child's age, stage, and unique needs.

Who we support

Challenges we help children & teens with

From big feelings to big transitions—specialized support for children, teens, and the families who love them.

Anxiety & worry

Worries, panic, and racing thoughts that make school, friendships, and daily life feel heavier than they need to be.

Emotion regulation

Big feelings, meltdowns, and shutdowns—building tools that actually work at home, at school, and beyond.

Trauma & adverse experiences

Helping children and teens process difficult experiences in a safe, paced, and developmentally appropriate way.

School stress & avoidance

Academic pressure, school refusal, and confidence—so school doesn't have to feel impossible.

Self-esteem & identity

Self-worth, belonging, and growing into who they are—with less shame and more confidence.

Pregnancy, postpartum & parenting

Support for adults navigating the emotional challenges of pregnancy, postpartum adjustment, and the journey of parenthood.
