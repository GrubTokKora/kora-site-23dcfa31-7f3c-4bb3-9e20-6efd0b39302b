Our mission

Specialized therapy for your journey

Welcome to RZ Therapy Solutions PLLC. We're glad you're here. Our mission is to provide trauma-informed, neurodiversity-affirming, and person-centered care for children, adolescents, adults, and families. We aim to empower each person to heal, grow, and live authentically.

Guided by a strengths-based approach, we honor every individual's unique path toward well-being. Our team is committed to offering a safe, supportive, and compassionate space where you can feel understood and genuinely cared for. Whether you're beginning therapy or continuing your journey, we're here to walk with you every step of the way.

We're here to walk with you every step of the way. — Rebecca Ziskind, Owner, RZ Therapy Solutions

How we care

Three pillars of our practice

Care shaped around connection, identity, and the whole person—not a one-size-fits-all model.

Trauma-informed

We understand how experiences shape the nervous system, and we pace every session with safety, trust, and choice at the center.

Neurodiversity-affirming

We honor different ways of thinking, feeling, and being—helping kids and adults understand their brains rather than trying to "fix" them.

Person-centered

Every plan is collaborative. We walk alongside you at your pace, highlighting resilience and the strengths already present in you and your family.
