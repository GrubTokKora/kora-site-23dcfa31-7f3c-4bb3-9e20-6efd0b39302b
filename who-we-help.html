Rebecca and her team of counselors in Colchester, Connecticut work from a restorative lens—meaning they focus on connection, relationships, and emotional repair. She helps clients understand why they feel and act the way they do, and then gently guides them toward healthier patterns. Her strength-based framework means she sees what's right with you, not what's wrong. She highlights your resilience, creativity, natural abilities, and the strengths already present in your family system.

Our team is known for their calm presence, creative approach, and ability to make kids and teens feel truly understood. They bring a balance of structure and spontaneity—serious when it matters, playful when it helps, and always authentically human. Clients choose RZ Therapy Solutions because it offers:

  • A welcoming, down-to-earth therapeutic environment
  • A collaborative process where you're part of the team
  • Trauma-informed, restorative, developmentally attuned care
  • A connection-focused approach that honors each client's pace
  • Support grounded in empathy, curiosity, and genuine respect

Rebecca and her team of therapists are here to walk alongside you, your child, your teen, or your young adult as they grow into themselves with confidence, emotional insight, and healthier relationships.
