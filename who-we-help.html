Rebecca and her team of counselors in Colchester, Connecticut specialize in children and teens—working from a child-centered, restorative lens that focuses on connection, relationships, and emotional repair. Using approaches like Child Centered Play Therapy (CCPT), Parent-Child Interaction Therapy (PCIT), Trauma-Informed Child Parent Psychotherapy (CPP), CBT, and the ARC model, they help young clients understand why they feel and act the way they do, and gently guide them toward healthier patterns. Their strength-based framework means they see what's right with your child—highlighting resilience, creativity, and the natural strengths already present in your family.

Our team is known for their calm presence, playful energy, and ability to make children and teens feel truly understood. They bring a balance of structure and spontaneity—serious when it matters, playful when it helps, and always authentically human. Families choose RZ Therapy Solutions because it offers:

  • A welcoming, child-friendly therapeutic environment
  • Evidence-based modalities: CCPT, PCIT, CPP, CBT & the ARC model
  • Trauma-informed, neurodiversity-affirming, developmentally attuned care
  • A connection-focused approach that honors each child's pace
  • Genuine partnership with parents and caregivers throughout the process

Rebecca and her team are here to walk alongside your child or teen as they grow into themselves with confidence, emotional insight, and healthier relationships—and to support your whole family along the way.

If you're looking for a therapist in Colchester, Connecticut who truly understands the world of children and teens—someone who can connect with your child in a real, warm, and playful way—our team would love to help. We also welcome adults navigating pregnancy, postpartum, or the challenges of parenting. RZ Therapy Solutions PLLC is here to offer child-centered, family-focused, restorative support for the moments that feel overwhelming.
