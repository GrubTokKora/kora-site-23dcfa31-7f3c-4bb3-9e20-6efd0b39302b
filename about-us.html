About Us

A team of licensed counselors dedicated to warm, engaging, clinically grounded care for children, adolescents, adults, and families.

Licensed Professional Counselor · National Certified Counselor · Owner

Rebecca Ziskind, MS, LPC, NCC

Rebecca is the owner of RZ Therapy Solutions PLLC and a Licensed Professional Counselor holding a master's degree in counseling from the University of Saint Joseph. She specializes in working with children, adolescents, adults, and their families—helping them navigate the emotional, behavioral, and developmental challenges that arise along the journey of life.

Rebecca strives to create a supportive and engaging therapeutic environment where clients feel understood and empowered to build self-awareness, strengthen coping skills, and foster personal growth. Grounded in restorative approaches and a strength-based framework, she emphasizes collaboration and therapeutic connection as key components of healing.

Recognizing that people—especially children—often process experiences nonverbally, Rebecca integrates play, art, sand tray, and other creative modalities into her work, creating opportunities for clients to explore emotions and communicate in ways that feel natural, safe, and developmentally appropriate.

Licensed Master Social Worker

Ashlee Cannon, LMSW

Ashlee is a Licensed Master Social Worker with a master's degree in social work from the University of Saint Joseph. She specializes in supporting children, adolescents, and their families through emotional, social, and developmental challenges.

Rooted in strengths-based CBT and DBT principles, she integrates evidence-based strategies with a warm, holistic, and person-centered approach. Because children and teens often express their inner worlds nonverbally, Ashlee incorporates play, art, and creative expression to help them explore emotions, strengthen self-awareness, and build adaptive coping skills.

Ashlee works with clients navigating anxiety, depression, identity development, behavioral challenges, family stress, and other life transitions—with trauma-informed, developmentally appropriate care that honors each family's path toward growth.

Licensed Professional Counselor Associate

Madison Marcavage, LPCA

Madison Marcavage is a Licensed Professional Counselor Associate with a master's degree in clinical mental health counseling from Sacred Heart University. She specializes in working with children and adolescents navigating trauma, anxiety, depression, ADHD, OCD, and adjustment-related challenges.

Grounded in a person-centered approach, Madison integrates CBT and DBT with play, art, and expressive techniques to help young clients process emotions without pressure to "find the right words." She is passionate about emotional regulation, practical coping skills, and lasting resilience—and partners closely with parents and caregivers so progress continues beyond the therapy room.

Licensed Clinical Social Worker

Mackenzie Vigil, LCSW

Mackenzie Vigil is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker with a Master's degree in Social Work from Boston University. She specializes in working with children (ages 0–10), parents, and families navigating trauma, emotional and behavioral challenges, parenting stress, family transitions, and perinatal and postpartum mental health.

Mackenzie takes a relational, trauma-informed approach, incorporating play therapy and attachment-based interventions. Her advanced training includes Child First, Circle of Security, Trauma-Informed Child-Parent Psychotherapy (TI-CPP), and Perinatal Mental Health through Postpartum Support International (PSI).

With a warm, compassionate presence, she creates a judgment-free space where children and families feel seen, supported, and empowered—tailoring treatment to foster resilience, healing, and meaningful growth.
