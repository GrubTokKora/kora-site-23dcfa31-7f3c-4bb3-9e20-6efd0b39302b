About Us

Our team of licensed counselors provides child therapy, play therapy, teen therapy, and family therapy in Colchester, CT—with specialized parent support and mental health therapy Colchester CT families trust.

Licensed Professional Counselor · National Certified Counselor · Owner

Rebecca Ziskind, MS, LPC, NCC

Rebecca is the owner of RZ Therapy Solutions PLLC and a Licensed Professional Counselor holding a master's degree in counseling from the University of Saint Joseph. As a leading Child Therapist in Connecticut and practicing Child Therapist Colchester CT families depend on, she serves as a compassionate Child and Adolescent Therapist specializing in Child Therapy, Teen Therapy, and Family Therapy.

Rebecca's clinical work provides targeted Anxiety Therapy for Children, ADHD Therapy for Children, Trauma Therapy for Children, and Emotional Regulation Therapy. She pairs individual counseling with meaningful Parent Support and Parenting Support / Parenting Therapy to help caregivers navigate every phase of family life.

Recognizing that all people — especially children — process experiences nonverbally, Rebecca integrates Play Therapy, art, sand tray, and Attachment Therapy for Children and Families. Through Autism / Neurodiversity Affirming Therapy and expressive modalities, she helps young clients explore emotions and communicate safely and naturally.

Licensed Master Social Worker

Ashlee Cannon, LMSW

Ashlee is a Licensed Master Social Worker with a master's degree in social work from the University of Saint Joseph. Practicing as both a Child Therapist and Teen Therapist, she specializes in supporting children, adolescents, and families through emotional, social, and developmental challenges.

Rooted in strengths-based CBT and DBT principles, Ashlee serves as a caring Child Anxiety Therapist providing Behavioral Therapy for Children, Emotional Regulation Therapy, and expressive Play Therapy. She partners with parents through practical Parent Support to reinforce skills at home.

Ashlee works with young clients navigating anxiety, depression, ADHD, family stress, and transitions—delivering Autism / Neurodiversity Affirming Therapy and comprehensive Mental Health Therapy Colchester CT families can count on for genuine progress.

Licensed Professional Counselor Associate

Madison Marcavage, LPCA

Madison Marcavage is a Licensed Professional Counselor Associate with a master's degree in clinical mental health counseling from Sacred Heart University. Practicing as a skilled Child and Adolescent Therapist and Teen Therapist, she provides dedicated Child Therapy and Teen Therapy for young clients navigating trauma, anxiety, depression, ADHD, OCD, and adjustment challenges.

Grounded in a person-centered framework, Madison integrates CBT and the ARC model with Play Therapy, expressive arts, and Trauma Therapy for Children. She provides ADHD Therapy for Children, Emotional Regulation Therapy, and hands-on Parent Support so growth continues beyond the therapy room.

Licensed Clinical Social Worker

Mackenzie Vigil, LCSW

Mackenzie Vigil is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker with a Master's degree in Social Work from Boston University. As an experienced Child Therapist and family specialist, she works with young children (ages 0–10), parents, and families through Family Therapy, Attachment Therapy for Children and Families, and Behavioral Therapy for Children.

Mackenzie takes a relational, trauma-informed approach incorporating Play Therapy and attachment-based interventions. Her advanced training includes Child First, Circle of Security, Trauma-Informed Child-Parent Psychotherapy (TI-CPP), and Perinatal Mental Health through Postpartum Support International (PSI).

She provides dedicated Parent Support and Parenting Support / Parenting Therapy, creating a judgment-free space where children and families feel seen, supported, and empowered to build lasting emotional security.

Licensed Master Social Worker

Amy Bace, LMSW

Amy is a Licensed Master Social Worker with a master's degree from the University of Saint Joseph and a School Social Worker certificate. Specializing in Child Therapy and Teen Therapy, she supports young children starting in toddlerhood, adolescents, and parents through comprehensive Anxiety Therapy for Children, ADHD Therapy for Children, and Trauma Therapy for Children.

Amy provides strengths-based Play Therapy, Emotional Regulation Therapy, and Autism / Neurodiversity Affirming Therapy. Her clinical approach draws from Circle of Security, ARC, CBT, and Attachment Therapy for Children and Families to meet clients where they are developmentally.

Amy also provides thoughtful Behavioral Therapy for Children and collaborative Parenting Support / Parenting Therapy, partnering closely with caregivers to strengthen co-regulation and support sustainable growth.
